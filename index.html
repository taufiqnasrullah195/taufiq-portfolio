02 - About

A dependable pair of hands for the IT that has to keep working.

I am a Computer Science graduate with practical experience across IT support, network infrastructure, and cybersecurity. Most of what I enjoy sits at the unglamorous end of the job: tracing why a connection drops, writing down the fix so the next person does not have to rediscover it, and keeping a machine's health visible before it becomes somebody's emergency.
